----- Interview (about 5 questions; Nexus may ask up to 12–15) -----

NEXUS:  When you line the bank feed up with the GL, what actually tells you
        two rows are the same item?
USER:   Same dollar amount and the same reference number. The GL can post a day
        or two before or after the bank clears it — give me two business days
        of wiggle room.
        --> Canvas: Matcher keys = amount + reference; date window = 2 days.

NEXUS:  Sometimes one wire hits the bank but clears several receipts in the GL.
        How do you want those handled?
USER:   That's normal for us — one deposit can cover several receipts. Match
        them as a group. If cash is left over, park it as unapplied and call
        it out.
        --> Canvas: split / allocation matching; leftovers → exceptions.

NEXUS:  How do you check that the bank's running balance column is right?
USER:   I walk the statement top to bottom: yesterday's balance, plus deposits,
        minus withdrawals. If my total doesn't match what the bank printed,
        flag that line.
        --> Canvas: Math — running balance vs stated balance; flag gaps.

NEXUS:  What about checks or deposits that have been sitting uncleared?
USER:   Anything uncleared past thirty days is high risk. Cite the Treasury SOP
        so the reviewer knows why it showed up.
        --> Canvas: Decision — >30 days + uncleared → high risk, SOP cited.

NEXUS:  What should I hand you at the end?
USER:   A PDF audit report for the controller — match summary up front, sign-off
        at the end. I'll download it here. No Slack.
        --> Canvas: Output = PDF download only.

NEXUS:  Here's the draft: ingest bank and GL plus the SOP, match on amount and
        reference with a two-day window, group split deposits, check running
        balances, flag stale items, and produce a PDF. If this matches what
        you need, confirm the pipeline.
USER:   That's the process — let's run it.
